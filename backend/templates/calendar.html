How to book a room (Calendar)

Follow these steps to reserve a room using the Deschutes Hall Calendar page:

  • Select a room from the "Select Room" dropdown which will update the calendar with accurate availability for that room for the current week by default (navigate to different weeks using the arrows next to the "Week of" field)
  • Choose your date and time using the dropdowns below "Start Time - End Time" OR by clicking a start and end cell for your desired timeslot
  • Fill in the rest of the details for your booking, and make any modifications if needed
  • Click Book Room. If there is a conflict, read the message and adjust your time or room accordingly
  • You will get an email sent to your uoregon.edu account confirming the booking. Modify or delete your booking on the "My Bookings" page
